What to Look For

When shopping an online fashion retailer, compare the exact product description, sizing information, material composition, shipping details and return policy. These details can matter as much as the overall design when deciding whether a piece is right for you.

Choosing the Right Piece

Start with items that fit your existing wardrobe and can be worn in more than one setting. Check measurements rather than relying only on your usual size, particularly when buying from a brand or retailer for the first time.

Before You Order

Review the current product page, total price, delivery timing and applicable return conditions. Promotions and availability can change, so the information displayed at checkout should be treated as the final reference.
