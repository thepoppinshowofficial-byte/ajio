Sleepwear Designed for Comfort

Sleepwear is one of those wardrobe categories where fabric, fit and construction can make a noticeable difference to the overall experience.

When shopping for sleepwear, consider how the fabric feels against the skin, how much room you prefer and whether the design works for the way you normally sleep and relax.

Loungewear for Everyday Moments

Loungewear can bridge the gap between traditional sleepwear and everyday casual clothing. Relaxed pants, tops and lightweight layers can work around the house or during slower parts of the day.

Neutral colors and simple silhouettes can also make individual pieces easier to combine with other items already in your wardrobe.

Choosing the Right Fabric

Fabric is an important consideration when selecting sleepwear. Different materials can feel noticeably different in terms of softness, weight, breathability and drape.

Before buying, check the product's current fabric composition and care instructions so you know how the garment should be washed and maintained.

How to Choose Loungewear

Think about where you plan to wear the pieces. If you want something primarily for sleeping, softness and comfort may be the priority.

If you want pieces that can also be worn while relaxing, traveling or working from home, consider the silhouette, structure and versatility of each item.

What Should You Check Before Buying?

Check the available sizes, measurements, fabric composition, care instructions, colors and current product availability.

It is also worth reviewing shipping, returns and the current price before completing your order.

Building a Relaxed Wardrobe

A small selection of well-chosen sleepwear and lounge pieces can create a versatile rotation without requiring a large wardrobe.

Consider choosing a few complementary colors and silhouettes that work together. This makes it easier to mix and match pieces throughout the week.
