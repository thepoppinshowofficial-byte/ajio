New Balance Guide: Sneakers, Running Shoes & Everyday Style

Updated August 2026 · FashionNewz Editorial
Quick Take: New Balance is a footwear and athletic brand known for running shoes, sneakers and lifestyle footwear. This guide looks at popular categories and what to consider when choosing a pair.

What Is New Balance?

New Balance is a footwear and athletic brand with products spanning running, training, walking and lifestyle footwear.

Its sneakers are also widely incorporated into everyday wardrobes, making the brand relevant to both performance-focused shoppers and people looking for casual footwear.

Running Shoes

Running shoes are designed around different types of running and individual preferences. Cushioning, fit, stability and weight can all influence the choice of shoe.

Before purchasing a running shoe, consider how and where you run, your preferred fit and the characteristics you want from the shoe.

Lifestyle Sneakers

Lifestyle sneakers can work as everyday footwear and can be paired with jeans, trousers, casual dresses and other wardrobe pieces.

When choosing a casual sneaker, consider the silhouette, color, materials and how easily it fits into your existing wardrobe.

Walking & Everyday Footwear

For everyday walking, comfort and fit are important considerations. Depending on your needs, you may also want to consider cushioning, support, flexibility and breathability.

How to Choose the Right New Balance Shoe

Start with the purpose of the shoe. A pair intended for running may have different characteristics from one designed primarily for casual wear.

Next, consider sizing, width, cushioning, materials, intended activity and personal comfort preferences.

What Should You Check Before Buying?

Review the current size guide, available widths, product specifications, materials, color options and current price.

It is also useful to check shipping, availability and the current return policy before completing your order.
