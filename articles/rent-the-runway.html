Rent the Runway Guide: Designer Rentals & Occasionwear

Updated August 2026 · FashionNewz Editorial
Quick Take: Rent the Runway is a fashion rental platform known for designer clothing and occasionwear. Renting can give shoppers access to different styles without purchasing every piece outright.

What Is Rent the Runway?

Rent the Runway is a fashion service focused on clothing rentals. The platform has become associated with designer fashion, special-event dressing and the idea of rotating a wardrobe without permanently purchasing every item.

Depending on the current assortment and available services, shoppers can explore different clothing styles for occasions ranging from weddings and parties to work events and everyday wear.

Designer Fashion Without a Permanent Purchase

One of the main ideas behind fashion rental is flexibility. Instead of buying an outfit for a single occasion, a shopper can consider renting a piece and returning it after the rental period.

This can be particularly appealing when you want to experiment with a different designer, silhouette or statement look.

Occasionwear

Special occasions are one of the areas where fashion rental can be especially useful. Weddings, formal celebrations, parties and other events may call for an outfit that is different from your everyday wardrobe.

When choosing an occasionwear piece, consider the dress code, event location, season, fit and expected weather.

Choosing the Right Rental

Start by considering the event and the type of outfit you want. Then review the item's current size information, measurements, material details and rental terms.

It is also useful to check the current availability and delivery information before committing to a particular item.

What Should You Check Before Renting?

Before placing an order, review the current rental price, available sizes, rental period, delivery timing, return instructions and any applicable fees.

For an important event, allow enough time for delivery and review the current policies so you understand the process before ordering.

Who Might Like Rent the Runway?

Fashion shoppers who enjoy trying different looks or need outfits for occasional events may find fashion rental particularly appealing.

It can also be useful for shoppers who prefer variety and don't want every special-occasion outfit to become a permanent part of their wardrobe.
