Power Tools

Power tools can make many construction, woodworking and home-improvement tasks more efficient. Depending on the project, shoppers may look at drills, saws, sanders, grinders and other equipment.

When choosing a power tool, consider the project, power requirements, compatibility, ergonomics and the manufacturer's specifications.

Hand Tools

Hand tools remain useful for repairs, maintenance and workshop projects. Common categories include wrenches, screwdrivers, pliers, hammers and measuring tools.

Choosing the right tool depends on the specific task, required size and intended frequency of use.

Outdoor Equipment

Outdoor projects can require specialized equipment and tools. Before purchasing, consider the type of project, operating requirements and storage needs.

Choosing Tools for Your Project

Start by identifying exactly what you need the tool to accomplish. Then compare specifications, compatibility, size, power requirements and included accessories.

For larger purchases, it can also be useful to compare warranties, manufacturer support and current return policies.

What Should You Check Before Buying?

Review the product specifications, dimensions, power source, compatibility, included components, current price and availability.

Also check shipping information, warranty details and the current return policy before completing your order.
