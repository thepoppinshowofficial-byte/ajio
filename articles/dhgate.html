What Should You Check Before Buying?

When shopping through a marketplace, review the individual product listing carefully. Look at the seller information, product description, measurements, available variations and current customer reviews.

Also check the current price, shipping information, estimated delivery time, return conditions and applicable buyer protections before completing an order.

Why Compare Sellers?

Marketplace shopping can provide access to products from different sellers. Comparing listings can help shoppers evaluate differences in price, specifications, reviews and shipping.

Taking a few extra minutes to compare similar listings can help you make a more informed purchasing decision.
